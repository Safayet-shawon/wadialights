I.c Lighting planner

How much light does your room need?

Enter the room. The planner sizes the fixtures with the lumen method, then solves a matrix least-squares problem so every lamp's output is tuned for an even result, and estimates the energy and running cost, with and without automation.

Automation saving is an assumption you can change. Results are an estimate for planning, not a certified lighting design.
