      I.c Lighting planner

      How much light does your room need?

      Enter the room. The planner sizes the fixtures with the lumen method, then solves a matrix least-squares problem so every lamp's output is tuned for an even result, and estimates the energy and running cost, with and without automation.

      Automation saving is an assumption you can change. Results are an estimate for planning, not a certified lighting design.

        II The craft

        Honest materials, patiently made.

        A good lamp is judged at the hour when everything else has gone quiet. We begin with that hour and work backwards.

        Editorial placeholder copy

        1. Specimen 01

          Glass

          Frosted and softly clouded, so a filament becomes a steady glow rather than a point of glare.

        2. Specimen 02

          Brass

          Turned, bevelled and left to age. Brass is never finished; it simply becomes more itself.

        3. Specimen 03

          Patina

          Warm hands and long evenings leave their mark. We design for the patina, not against it.

        4. Specimen 04

          Walnut & linen

          Dark timber to anchor the light, pale linen to let it breathe, and nothing in between that does not need to be there.

        A lamp is not an object. It is the way a room feels at nine o’clock on a winter evening.
