      II The craft

      Materials that shape the glow.

      A good lamp is judged at the hour when everything else has gone quiet. These are the materials we look at first when choosing a light.

      Sample photographs. Ask us about the finishes we stock.

      1. Specimen 01

        Glass

        Frosted and softly clouded, so a filament becomes a steady glow rather than a point of glare.

      2. Specimen 02

        Brass

        Warm to look at and long lasting. Brass is never quite finished; it simply becomes more itself.

      3. Specimen 03

        Patina

        Warm hands and long evenings leave their mark. A good finish is chosen to age well.

      4. Specimen 04

        Walnut & linen

        Dark timber to anchor the light, pale linen to let it breathe, and nothing in between that does not need to be there.

      A lamp is not an object. It is the way a room feels at nine o’clock on a winter evening.
